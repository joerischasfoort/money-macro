## The truth about Europe’s mass migration experience

Okay, to truly understand the impact migration has had on Europe, we need to go back in time. Europe has essentially seen 5 big migration waves. 

The first wave was the so-called guestworker wave that was driven by the need for labor in Western Europe’s fast growing economies. First, there were Italian, Spanish and Greek migrants. Then, when those economies started growing themselves, Western Europe started attracting low-skilled workers from [Turkey](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/17503280.2023.2253574#abstract), Morocco and Yugoslavia.

This program stopped after the oil crisis of the 1970s, when many jobs disappeared, but many guestworkers stayed. On top of that, migration continued for a while as guest workers brought their families. 

The second wave was tied to decolonialization from the 1950s to 1980s. During this wave, many refugees from newly independent colonies migrated to their former colonial masters. This wave was possible because during this era many colonial subject were technically nationals, and since they often already spoke the language, it was relatively easy for them to move. This is why today there still many Indonesian descendants in the Netherlands, Algerians and Africans in France, and Indians and Pakistani’s in the UK. 

Third, after the fall of the iron curtain, there was a massive migration wave from East to Western Europe. Fourth, in 2015 and 2016, there was a mass migration wave from Syria, North Africa and Afghanistan to Europe, and finally, in the last few years millions of Ukrainians migrated to Europe. 

Each of these migration waves had their own political backlashes. But, do people like Trump and JD Vance refer to Eastern Europeans moving to Western Europe when they talk about civilizational erasure?

## Could it still be true that immigrants are making Europe far less safe?

This is where we get back to Trump’s UN speech. 

“””In Switzerland, beautiful Switzerland, it's 72% of the people in prisons are from outside of Switzerland.“””

First, Trump is not wrong. Immigrants ARE overrepresented in European prisons. But, as you can see here, Switzerland is an extreme case. In his speech, Trump DOES NOT mention that in some striking cases, like the case of the UK, immigrants are actually UNDERREPRESENTED in prison, just as they are in the other Anglo-Saxon countries like US, Australia and New Zealand. 

So, what is going on? 

First, as you can see here, Anglo-Saxon countries tend to attract much more highly educated migrations. These are more likely to find a high paying job and therefore less likely to commit crimes and end up in jail. 

So, the fact that continental European prisons have a lot of immigrants is probably because Europe’s immigrants are simply poorer than the average European, it does NOT necessarily have anything to do with their cultural background. 

A second powerful explanation is that Europeans are pretty old, and imm[igrants are on aver](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/products-eurostat-news/w/ddn-20250221-2?utm_source=chatgpt.com)age much younger. 

Younger peo[ple commit](https://www.journals.uchicago.edu/doi/pdf/10.1086/737409?download=true) more crimes. So, again, this could disprove the narrative that the type of immigration matters. 

Finally, being IN JAIL more could be because immigrants commit more crimes. But, there is also credible research which shows that immigrants are more likely to be held in jail while waiting for the trial because judge[s often believe they are](https://academic.oup.com/jleo/article-abstract/34/4/511/5100740?redirectedFrom=fulltext) a higher flight risk than locals which, makes sense I guess, but that puts them in a worse situation for negotiating deals, especially if they are not fluent [in a European lang](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.10685)uage.

That being said, nobody knows for sure how BIG these 3 effects are. So, all of this research NEITHER PROVES that immigrants are innocent NOR that they are GUILTY of committing more crimes. 

But, okay, that means Trump and Orban COULD still be right. Migrants COULD make Europe much less safe. We need more evidence.

To separate the effect of migrants from broader effects that are decreasing crime overall, like Europeans g[etting old](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/Papers.cfm?abstractid=2566965)er and better home and story s[ecurity syst](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10610-010-9130-y)ems, researchers have done several detailed studies about the relationship between crime and migration in the [US](https://www.annualreviews.org/content/journals/10.1146/annurev-criminol-032317-092026), [Italy](https://academic.oup.com/jeea/article-abstract/10/6/1318/2299419) and [Germany](https://www.econstor.eu/handle/10419/201922).

“””in general the conclusion is that this increases in migration, sometimes very huge increases in migration, they have NO significant effect on crime rates,“””

For example, as you can see here, while MOST migrants came to East Germany between 2008 and 2019, crime has mostly gone up in West and South Germany. 

But if migrants do NOT clearly increase overall crime, even in places like Germany and Italy, how can low skilled immigrants be so over-represented in prisons? 

When, we asked Professor Pinotti about this, he offered another explanation: immigrants may be REPLACING native-born offenders in some types of criminal activity.

“””So think about the drug sector, drug trafficking which is a major criminal enterprise in most cases, right? If you buy drugs, now in many cases, the dealer is gonna be a member of some foreign criminal group, right? But in the end, the amount of drugs that is sold in the street is still gonna be the same. It depends more on the demand for drugs,“””

It is possible that YES immigrants commit more crimes than native people. And, yet they DO NOT increase crime overall. Just like immigrants make take low-skilled jobs like fruit picking from native Europeans, they may actually also simply take crime jobs from native Europeans as well.

Still, I have to be honest, we don’t have enough data to completely confirm that this is what happened. But, it could explain why even in detailed studies, big migration waves do NOT seem to lead to more crime, but they could lead to a higher number of criminals in jail being immigrants.

No. Just because the truth is not one extreme DOES not mean that the truth is the other extreme. Immigrants from distant cultures always [causes tens](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/15562948.2025.2543545#d1e154)ions. But, one key factor that often gets overlooked is access to jobs.

If asylum seekers and migrants are not allowed to work LEGALLY, they can end up stuck. Their visa might expire. They might not have money to return home. And without access to regular jobs, some may turn to illegal ways of making money, like drug dealing or burglary. The work of professor Pinotti has shown [tha](https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257%2Faer.20150355&utm_source=chatgpt.com)t:

“””when immigrants receive legal status, they are finally able to obtain legal status and therefore the right to work in the official economy, the probability of committing crimes decreases by 50%. It's cut by half, right?“””

So, the take-away is that IF YOU DO want to have some migration, it’s essential to do it ORDERLY. This can also explain that a [recent study](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0927537123001410) did find that ONE YEAR AFTER the relatively chaotic 2015-2016 migration wave THERE WAS ACTUALLY a significant wave of increased burglaries and theft in Germany. So, there was SOME truth to what the AfD politician said to Elon Musk. 

Another thing is that you want to make sure that migrants AND THEIR CHILDREN are able to integrate into society, and that they do NOT all end up in isolated ghetto’s such as the French banlieues, or in Brussels suburbs like Anderlecht. 

On top of that, one major limitation of the research we have discussed today, is that it looks at migrants… but not at their children. [Recent research](https://elisajacome.github.io/Jacome/intergen_immigrants_15_countries.pdf) shows that in countries like Austria, the Netherlands, France, and Denmark, children of immigrants from the first two waves often have a harder time moving up in life compared to children of native parents who grew up with the same level of poverty.

This may help explain why second-generation immigrants in, for example, Denmark ARE ACTUALLY more likely to end up in prison than both their parents AND native Danish children.

Finally, we should be open to the possibility that men from countries with more traditional attitudes towards women are, on average, more likely to engage in sexual violence. This should not be entirely surprising since we know that men with sexist attitudes are [more likely](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s12119-023-10127-6) to commit violence against women. Worryingly, a recent study from Lund University found that [63 percent of those convicted of rape in Sweden have an immigrant background](https://portal.research.lu.se/en/activities/63-percent-of-those-convicted-of-rape-in-sweden-have-an-immigrant/#:~:text=%22If%20you%20were%20born%20abroad,Social). This MASSIVE over-representation decreased a bit after controlling for socioeconomic factors, substance abuse, and mental health conditions, BUT it did NOT disappear COMPLETELY.
